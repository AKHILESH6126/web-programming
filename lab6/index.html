About us

We are a small group of stargazers who set up camp far from city lights. Every site is picked for its clear horizon and quiet, and every night is paced so nobody gets cold or tired.

Groups never top twelve people, so everyone gets a turn at the telescope and time to ask questions.

What to bring

  • Warm layers, even in summer
  • A red-light torch (it keeps your night vision)
  • A reclining chair or thick mat
  • A flask of something hot
